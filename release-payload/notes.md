## Spektrafilm 9.49 · Negs / Fotufilm

ARM64 Android APK, Android 7.0+ (API 24). Same app/signing identity as 9.48.

- **Negs · Fotufilm** shelf: 30 film models and one paper, represented by 41 profiles/development variants. CineStill 800T/400D, Phoenix 200, Aerochrome, Acros II, Astia, Eterna/Reala, Agfachrome, Kodachrome, LomoChrome and more. Approximate, creative and estimated-time models are labelled.
- **Подготовка / Prep:** exposure, highlights, shadows, contrast, lift, gain, Color Boost, saturation, midtone detail. Before film, for preview and export, with Undo/reset.
- Stock-specific grain/DIR, four-layer BW support, real BB3200 reference, favourites, multiword search, Negs preset mapping and film-format choices.
- Existing profiles and original film-edge frames retained. Russian interface.

Numerical/native, frontend and desktop/phone Chromium checks passed. No physical-device performance or image-quality test was available.

Selected features/data are ported, not the whole Negs application. Full scope and limitations: [NEGS_INTEGRATION.md](https://github.com/komarpunk-design/Spektrakrill/blob/feature/negs-tools-and-stocks/docs/NEGS_INTEGRATION.md).
